Bless Content StudioSandbox testing

Testing terms

Last updated: 10 September 2026. Bless Content Studio is an experimental web application operated by Mbabazi Blessing in Uganda for authorized TikTok sandbox testing.

Available service

This version connects an eligible TikTok sandbox account, previews an original MP4, and submits an explicitly approved private test post. It does not provide automatic video generation, scheduled publishing, public posting, paid-partnership posting or a public creator service. It is not represented as endorsed by TikTok or approved for production Direct Post use.

Your account and content

Connect only an account you own and are authorized to use for this test. Keep your credentials private. Upload only content you have the necessary rights and permissions to share, including music, images and any people represented. Follow TikTok’s applicable terms and community rules and use accurate AI and promotional disclosures.

Approval and publishing

Review the video, account, caption, visibility and disclosures before consenting. Clicking Upload & post to Only me authorizes transfer to TikTok and a private posting attempt. Uploading can trigger publication after TikTok processing; it is not merely saving a draft. TikTok may reject, delay, restrict or remove content. The service cannot guarantee reach, account eligibility, publication or uninterrupted availability.

Testing limitations

Sessions last no more than one hour, and uploads are limited to MP4 files up to 20 MiB. Keep your original files. If a request is interrupted, check its status and TikTok before attempting another post. Disconnection ends local session access and requests token revocation; it does not remove already submitted TikTok posts.

Support and privacy

Read the privacy notice for how data is processed and removed. For support or a deletion request, use the operator’s contact page. Features or these testing terms may change as development proceeds; review the terms when using a new version.
