Bless Content StudioSandbox testing

Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 September 2026. This notice describes the private sandbox testing version of Bless Content Studio, operated by Mbabazi Blessing in Uganda.

Information used

When you connect TikTok, the app receives your TikTok account identifier, display name, avatar, granted permissions and access token. It queries creator information, including your username, posting options and maximum video duration, to show the correct posting controls. Your password is entered on TikTok, not collected by this app.

When you approve an upload, the app sends your selected video, caption, visibility, interaction settings and content disclosures to TikTok. Selecting and previewing a video alone does not upload it. The app also uses random login-state values, session identifiers, anti-forgery tokens and upload identifiers to secure the flow and check status.

Storage and providers

The website and server functions run on Cloudflare. TikTok access tokens and private media-link details are encrypted before storage in Cloudflare D1. The browser receives a secure HTTP-only session cookie, not the TikTok token. After approval, video bytes are temporarily stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket so TikTok can download the file through a short-lived, unguessable link on this website. Video bytes are not stored in D1 or as public website files. Captions are not retained in an app content library. Refresh tokens are discarded in this testing version.

Cloudflare and TikTok process data needed to provide their services, potentially outside Uganda. Cloudflare may process request information such as IP addresses, request URLs and operational logs. The app does not intentionally log tokens, authorization codes or private media links. Provider logs and backups follow the settings and retention practices of those services.

Expiry and deletion

Login-state records expire after ten minutes. App sessions and upload records expire after no more than one hour. Temporary R2 video objects are deleted after TikTok reports a final result, when the session is disconnected, or during expiry cleanup. A one-day R2 lifecycle rule is used as a deletion backstop. Expired D1 records are no longer accepted and are removed on the next Studio request; they may remain stored until that cleanup occurs. Disconnect & clear session removes the current session and its upload records and requests revocation of TikTok access. If TikTok cannot confirm revocation, the app explains that you should remove its access in TikTok’s app permissions.

Disconnecting does not delete videos already sent to TikTok. Manage those posts within TikTok. Removing access from TikTok prevents further authorized API use but does not instantly erase app records; the expiry and cleanup process still applies.

Your choices and contact

You can decline login, choose not to upload, disconnect, or request help with access or deletion through Mbabazi Blessing’s contact page. Identify the TikTok account concerned; never send passwords, access tokens or client secrets. Requests involving records in another active session may require verification of account ownership.

Changes

This notice must be updated before features that change data handling, such as long-term scheduling or a public creator service, are introduced.
